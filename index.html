  • Click "Generate Character" to create your character, repeat to populate new random options.
  • Click "Reset Selections" to clear all fields and selections
  • Click "Lock Character" to copy the generated character into the form.
    • Once locked, you can manually edit any field.
    • After locking, delete the text and select random if you would like to generate new random options.
